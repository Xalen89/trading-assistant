4. Scalping
Core Mechanics
Scalping is an ultra-fast strategy focused on capturing tiny price moves repeatedly throughout the trading day. Scalpers focus on immediate market microstructure and liquidity imbalances rather than long-term chart trends.
Timeframe: 5-second, 15-second, or 1-minute tick charts.
Holding Period: Seconds to a few minutes.
How It Looks On Your Screen
Workstation Layout: Dominated by a Direct Market Access (DMA) Order Entry Ticket, a Level 2 depth-of-market ladder, and real-time Time & Sales stream.
Chart Setup: Very few indicators; focus is on tick-by-tick order flow and sudden shifts in bid/ask sizing.
Key Execution Rules
1. Entry: Buy at the Bid price when a large buyer wall appears on Level 2 or heavy green prints sweep the Tape.
2. Stop Loss: Tight stop loss (often 2–5 ticks) placed immediately upon execution.
3. Exit: Limit order placed 3 to 10 ticks higher to lock in immediate gains before price momentum slows.
