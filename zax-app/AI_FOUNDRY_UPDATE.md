# AI, learning and strategy workbench update

Not every requested capability is complete or statistically validated. This version adds a usable private AI coach, structured Library analysis, runnable quantitative research, the approved two paper modes, and practical learning/discipline tools. It does not claim an unrestricted AI, a perfect trader, validated profitability or brokerage execution.

## Where to use the new features

Open **AI** from Chart or **Ask my AI** from Strategies. The owner-only coach answers trading, strategy, confluence and pair questions using selected available rules, actual read-only quote/candle facts, recent research and canonical paper history. Source references and context status distinguish supplied facts from general educational explanations. Conversation is retained locally under the owner/scope and failed messages can be retried. Questions about economic/COT context can retrieve the available dated official records within a bounded lookup. The coach cannot execute trades, change risk limits, approve its own rules or manufacture missing prices/statistical evidence.

In **My Library**, an uploaded compatible source is preserved privately and receives the existing extraction plus a structured scan. The structured result separates trigger, named confluences, source excerpts, indicator parameters, entry, invalidation, targets, risk, required data and ambiguity. It is retained as an unapproved source-review revision; a failed second scan does not discard the original. Open **Library AI** on a source to reanalyze or ask about it. Accepted candidate styles are saved inactive and still require supported deterministic rules/data and a separately approved confluence configuration before strategy-mode paper use. An image/prose reference is not automatically a validated execution detector, and uploaded code is not run.

The **Strategies → Quant model studio** replaces the five static prerequisite cards. Run synchronized pairs/OLS-spread research, selected-universe momentum, public Coinbase venue-depth quote/inventory research, systematic trend research, or a causal chronological ML forward-return classifier. Inputs come from supported real history or explicitly labelled owner CSV/JSON imports. Set lookback, costs and model parameters, run, inspect source/as-of, diagnostics, signals and qualification, then retry or edit. The macro family currently implements the trend component; it does not invent economic release vintages. Market making produces tick/lot-constrained paper quote plans from a real single-venue book—not simulated fills, queues or PnL. Multi-leg plans are research output, not a two-leg order ticket.

## Paper practice and strategy mode

Buy/Sell now offers the chosen **Manual practice** and **Gated strategy** modes. Practice retains canonical fresh quote, risk, geometry, sizing and drawdown checks; it is not represented as a valid strategy signal. Strategy mode additionally requires an explicitly approved current-version source-bound trigger and weighted gate, with enabled pending/stale data blocking entry. The server independently reanalyzes rather than trusting a client “passed” flag. Configure and approve a supported native gate in Strategies before using that mode.

Every new paper record carries mode, causal entry features and condition/score/gate snapshots. A strategy pending limit is re-gated at its actually observed fill with the same saved strategy/configuration versions; placement approval alone cannot fill it. Practice pending limits can open from fresh side quotes without silently enabling auto bracket management. Open paper positions also record observed quote excursions while the app samples; these are not a complete tick path, broker fill history or tick-complete MAE/MFE.

## Learn and protect the process

**Trading foundations** and **Learning review** are in Library and the coach. Lessons cover risk before entry, trigger versus confluence, execution costs, uncertainty, loss-chasing and review. Canonical trade checks flag rapid re-entry after losses, risk escalation and overtrading as observations—not a diagnosis. An optional **Personal post-loss pause** in ticket Advanced is saved explicitly and enforced by the paper server; it is not secretly enabled for every user or described as proof of readiness.

Learning analytics compare actual eligible closed outcomes by score, condition presence/absence and regime, with missing legacy snapshots explicit. Rates/effect conclusions stay withheld when the required per-comparison sample is insufficient. Correlation findings and rule-change proposals remain descriptive, version-aware and for human review; nothing self-promotes into a validated win model.

## Alerts and public market context

Alerts can select market, frame, strategy, minimum points/count, and trigger-below-gate conditions. Owner foreground scanning and chart navigation are wired. **Native closed-app remote push is still unactivated**: it needs successful backend deployment/scheduling, a genuine signed/development native build, Expo/APNs/FCM setup, permission/token enrollment and an actual delivery test. Safari/Home Screen and this source export do not prove that native push path.

Where the earlier optional context provider is absent, the app now has a no-key official **US-only** release-schedule fallback using BLS/BEA/Federal Reserve and a dated **CME Bitcoin futures-only** CFTC COT record. Impact importance is app-authored, not an agency rating. There are no invented consensus/actual/surprise values, FOMC publication times or spot/OTC/crypto-wide positions. Partial coverage is reported, and the COT report date is not called live positioning. Official source details are in `research/public-context-sources.md`.

## What is still needed

Statistical qualification cannot be switched on by publishing. It still requires suitable real data, enough defined outcomes, costed point-in-time evaluation, walk-forward and untouched holdout evidence, and hypothesis controls. Current exploratory forward-return/OLS/trend models are not a calibrated whole-strategy win classifier. A survivorship-free total-return universe, historical macro vintages, licensed non-Coinbase depth/NBBO, queue/latency/fill evidence, full trade/tick/TCA store, calibrated gradient-boosted win model and actual multi-leg execution remain unmet scope; their absence is not disguised by a working Run button.

The app remains owner-only and paper-only. The current access surface is temporary Preview. Permanent publication and canonical checkpoint saving remain blocked by the previously reported recovery/transport issue; no permanent URL or signed iOS app is claimed. Source exports are not installed-device, owner paper-submit or push-delivery proof. Please test the coach, one source scan, and both paper modes on the actual phone; gated waiting is correct when its real conditions are not met.

## Verification evidence

The current source has passed TypeScript/lint, full mock/synthetic-fixture unit checks, backend build, and web/iOS/Android source export. Exact latest counts are recorded in `LATEST_FOUNDRY_UPDATE.md`; one pre-existing auth integration test remains skipped. A real read-only diagnostic retrieved 400 completed BTC/ETH daily bars, ran the research modules, received a genuine managed AI reply and connected to the official Coinbase `level2_batch` stream. A targeted maker check produced actual-book tick/lot-constrained quote targets after correcting request-start/receipt clock ordering. These tests establish connectivity and source/code behavior, not edge or user-account order execution.

Source-specific software gaps also remain: the exact requested VWAP/STDV variant is not replaced by the legacy range-target model; the current pairs lab reports OLS/residual diagnostics rather than a formal Engle–Granger/Johansen test; the macro lab is trend-only without release-vintage/rate-differential logic; and the classifier is a causal forward-return research baseline, not the requested calibrated gradient-boosted trade-win model. A general Library rule interpreter and complete two-leg paper execution/TCA are not supplied by blueprint extraction. These are implementation/data work, not features that become complete simply by publishing. The app labels these distinctions instead of removing a warning and calling them validated.
