# MACD family audit

**Scope/status.** Source review only; no synthetic or performance validation. MACD is reference, not proven edge.

**Implemented.** `lib/trading/github-presets.ts` (lines 50–81) declares MACD 12/26/9: completed-close MACD-over-signal cross; reverse cross is review/exit context, not a short or automatic exit. `lib/trading/indicators.ts` (67–97, 179–182) computes SMA-seeded EMA12/26 and signal-9, requiring 35 closed bars for current/prior comparison; more history is needed for convergence. `lib/trading/github-setups.ts` (50–75, 98–118) detects/display-steps for the long cross but always returns `ready:false`; risk/execution remains unknown. This is an app-authored close-cross variant, not the source normalized-threshold method. Manual weighted confirmations in `lib/trading/engine.ts` (14–15) are not data-derived confluence scoring. Trades store met-step names, not timestamped per-condition source/state/score (`server/trading/paper-service.ts:188–208`; `server/trading/validation.ts:10`). `python/research_engine.py:14–18,38–54` and `lib/trading/research.ts:41–63` support MA-cross/FVG, not MACD; they cannot validate this family.

**Real data / limits.** `server/trading/tradelocker.ts:70–71` supplies selected-account REST bid/ask and broker-defined history (up to 600 requested bars); candles are polled, not an exchange WebSocket. `server/trading/market.ts:16,29–31` supplies public Coinbase ticker/candles (up to 300 bars); it is Coinbase-specific, not consolidated-market data. `lib/trading/indicators.ts:167–173` explicitly treats non-Coinbase volume semantics as unknown. No confirmed L2, NBBO, 5–10-year macro history, point-in-time total-return/survivorship-free universe, or ≥100 MACD outcomes exists. Volume is not broker exchange volume.

**Falsifiable confluence CANDIDATES (untested; use only completed bars).** (1) Higher-timeframe alignment: 1h MACD>signal at latest closed 1h bar, same selected feed. (2) Zero-line state: MACD>0 at trigger close. (3) Volatility state: ATR14 at trigger close versus median of prior 20 completed ATR14 values, same feed/timeframe; preregister threshold/direction. (4) Coinbase-only activity: completed-bar volume ≥1.5× mean of preceding 20 completed same-timeframe volumes; never label broker volume equivalent. Candidate thresholds are hypotheses, not recommendations; test correlation and flag any pair >0.9.

**Executable choices/gates.** Before testing, freeze entry (e.g. next-bar open after close-confirmed cross), long-only versus separately specified short, stop/target or time exit, reverse-cross behavior, timeframe/universe, costs/spread/slippage, and missing/stale-data treatment. Require ≥100 actual outcomes per setup before any edge claim; chronological walk-forward folds, a latest untouched holdout, and multiple-hypothesis correction across all tried rules/weights. Log timestamped source and each condition’s state at entry; demote failed OOS conditions to watch. Until these and cost-aware OOS/holdout results exist: **insufficient evidence; no promotion or trading recommendation.**
