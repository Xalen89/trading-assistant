# Core Sequence audit

**Scope:** Core Sequence only; source evidence is the supplied Foundry/confluence specs and cited app files. No synthetic-fixture validation or trading recommendation is claimed.

**Implemented.** `lib/trading/setup-presets.ts:22-69` defines Core Sequence as a *reference* rulebook, not a user-authored strategy. `lib/trading/setup-engine.ts:91-97,144-225,176-205,228-253,284-630` implements closed-bar OHLC detection: six completed hourly bars give directional context; a prior swing requires two later closed bars to confirm, then sweep/rejection; post-sweep directional body must be at least prior ATR and close through the local structural level; a regular three-bar FVG or last-opposite-candle body midpoint supplies a POI, followed by a later closed-bar CE touch. Core accepts either FVG or OB. Entry/stop/2R target references are proposals, not orders. `setup-engine.ts:1157-1174` exposes step statuses, but not weighted scoring. Preset factors all default to weight 10 (`setup-presets.ts:7-12`); there is no configurable weight/gate, per-entry confluence snapshot, contribution analytics, or OOS promotion workflow in this engine. The confluence spec is requirements prose, not implementation.

**Feed and limits.** Per task, available real feeds are selected TradeLocker-account broker quotes/history and public Coinbase crypto candles/ticker. Core uses candles; no confirmed L2, direct-exchange feed for broker instruments, historical NBBO, 5–10-year macro/release series, survivorship-free point-in-time universe, or ≥100 setup outcomes is established. Broker OHLC volume semantics are explicitly unverified; Coinbase asset volume is venue-specific, not consolidated/exchange-wide volume (`lib/trading/indicators.ts:167-173`).

**Falsifiable confluence candidates—not validated recommendations:** (1) completed broker D1 close above/below SMA20, with SMA20 slope over five completed D1 bars; (2) execution-timeframe ATR14 percentile versus prior 100 completed bars, threshold fixed before testing; (3) for Coinbase crypto only, closed-candle volume ≥2× preceding 20 same-frame candles (venue-volume hypothesis, not transferable broker volume); (4) selected-account quote spread in bps below a predeclared instrument-specific cap, with quote age ≤30s. Store point-in-time values/states; historical spread testing awaits archived quotes. Test pairwise correlations and flag >0.9; core sequence components themselves overlap and may double-count.

**Validation gate.** Require ≥100 independent qualified outcomes per rule/asset scope before any edge claim; closed-bar, next-bar execution and costs; rolling walk-forward train/test, untouched latest holdout never tuned on, and correction/reporting for all tried hypotheses. Demote failed OOS conditions to watch. Existing `setup-engine.ts:870-973,1245-1269` caps retrieval at 100 cases, reports a rate at ≥30 resolved cases, and omits spread/fees/slippage; this is not a 100-sample gate or OOS validation. `python/research_engine.py:14-18,38-54,90-120` supports only MA-cross/FVG baselines, not Core Sequence.
