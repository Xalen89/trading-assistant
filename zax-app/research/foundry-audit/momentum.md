Actual neighboring detectors are a single-asset, long-only EMA20/50 pullback and an intraday breakout (`lib/trading/indicator-setups.ts:83–200,201–303`); neither ranks assets or tests time-series returns. `engine.ts:33–40` reports descriptive single-series momentum only. `python/research_engine.py:14–36,38–54` supports ≤2 series and MA-cross/FVG only, not momentum ranking.

Neither feed establishes a point-in-time, survivorship-free total-return universe, delisted assets, corporate actions/dividends, or 5–10-year history. No confirmed L2/NBBO or macro-release history; L2/NBBO is not essential to this family. No ≥100 real setup outcomes are established.
