# STDV manipulation — scope audit

**Verdict:** the supplied scope and app define different strategies. `requirements/foundry-scope-04.txt:137` specifies a rolling-VWAP, ±2.5σ mean-reversion model; `lib/trading/setup-presets.ts:163–190` instead defines “STDV” as a reference-only set of −2/−2.5/−4 manipulation-range Fibonacci targets, explicitly not statistical sigma or an entry model.

**What exists:** `lib/trading/setup-engine.ts:207–253,632–715` detects closed-bar prior-swing sweep/rejection, directional body ≥ prior ATR and structural close-through, then projects `A + r(B−A)` from displacement/sweep extremes. It filters forming bars (`:91–97`); the STDV candidate is never ready and has no entry/stop/target. STDV alone cannot produce a historical entry outcome (`:1190–92`); its levels may be selected as a paper-plan target if ≥2R (`:1388–1431`), not as an optimized edge. These detect components, not statistical STDV. Preset factors are references (`setup-presets.ts:14–18`); generic `engine.ts:14–15` scoring uses configured/manual confirmations, not these setup statuses or a mandatory STDV gate.

**Data/validation:** existing client hooks poll selected TradeLocker quotes every 15s and candles every 20s; Coinbase has public ticker WebSocket and candle history (`lib/trading/client.ts:11–15,28–50`). Broker volume semantics are explicitly unknown; Coinbase volume is provider asset volume, not consolidated volume (`indicators.ts:167–173`). No confirmed L2/NBBO, true trade-level VWAP, or source-verified broker traded volume. `python/research_engine.py:18–19,38–55` backtests only MA-cross/FVG. Setup history caps cases at 100 and reports a rate at 30 resolved samples (`setup-engine.ts:25,950–72`); neither establishes 100 STDV outcomes.

**Unvalidated confluence candidates** (strictly closed-bar/source timestamps; candidate definitions, not recommendations): (1) last six completed 1h closes trend in trade direction (TradeLocker selected-account or Coinbase candles); (2) same-source closed-bar volume ≥2× preceding 20-bar mean (Coinbase provider volume only; TradeLocker status pending); (3) sampled TradeLocker bid–ask spread ≤ a pre-registered ATR fraction at trigger time, with stale quote = pending (not NBBO); (4) training-only-defined ATR14/close volatility regime filter. Select either statistical VWAP-band/re-entry rules or fib-range targets before testing; do not conflate them. Predefine windows, entry, stop, exit, VWAP/volume semantics and costs. Test correlated conditions for >0.9 correlation; remove/demote duplicates.

**Promotion gates:** ≥100 independent realized outcomes per setup (otherwise insufficient data); chronological walk-forward with parameters fixed per next fold; untouched latest holdout never tuned; correct for all tried thresholds/features/hypotheses; report costs, expectancy and uncertainty. No validated candidates or live edge is claimed.
