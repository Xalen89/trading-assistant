# Bollinger Bands (BB) family audit

**Status: partial indicator reference, not a validated or executable strategy.** The user-provided `mean-reversion` reference covers 15m/1h/D and operationalizes long-only: previous completed low ≤ BB20 lower with RSI14≤30, then latest completed close re-enters the bands and RSI crosses above 30. It proposes entry at that close and target at SMA20; the fixed-distance wick stop is unspecified. Thus the detector leaves `ready=false`; entry/target fields are not an actionable order/alert. No shorts, divergence, or stop/sizing are inferred. (`lib/trading/indicator-presets.ts:125-176`; `lib/trading/indicator-setups.ts:304-370,373-403`.)

**Implemented:** BB20 is 20 completed closes’ SMA ±2 population standard deviations; RSI14 uses Wilder smoothing, with closed-bar filtering. The BB branch is an actual detector; source prose about divergence is not detected. (`lib/trading/indicators.ts:31-51,67-73,137-144`.) Inputs are supplied broker history/quotes or Coinbase candles; REST polling and a Coinbase ticker WebSocket are shown in `lib/trading/client.ts:11-15,28-50`. Broker/provider volume semantics are unverified, not exchange volume; Coinbase volume is venue asset volume, not consolidated stock volume (`indicators.ts:167-173`). Volume is not a BB rule input.

**Missing/data and rule choices:** no ≥100 BB outcomes, BB-specific historical evaluation, calibrated edge, or costs-aware BB backtest. Numerical-family outcomes are unconfigured; Python baseline accepts only MA-cross/FVG (`setup-engine.ts:1226-30`; `python/research_engine.py:14-18,120`). No confirmed 5–10-year history, L2/NBBO, or point-in-time total-return universe. Predeclare next-bar-open fills (not same-close), stop distance/buffer, costs, gap/ambiguous-bar and missing-bar rules, exit/holding horizon. Manual equal-weight confirmations are not logged/validated BB confluences.

**Falsifiable confluence candidates only—not tested recommendations**, on eligible 15m/1h/D completed bars: (1) prior low ≤ prior BB20 lower; (2) prior RSI14≤30 and current RSI14>30; (3) current close > current lower and < upper BB; (4) current BB width ≤ median of preceding 20 completed widths. Source is the supplied candle series, with explicit prior/current bar times; (4) is proposed, not implemented. Conditions (1)–(3) may overlap; test pairwise correlation and do not double-count >0.9-correlated conditions.

**Gates:** ≥100 actual non-overlapping outcomes; chronological walk-forward, frozen test-fold rules, untouched recent holdout, multiple-hypothesis correction across tested rules/thresholds/weights, costs/regime analysis, and per-entry condition-state logging. Failed OOS conditions become watch-only. No synthetic validation or performance claim. Missing sample, feed coverage and verified fill/stop model remain unavailable.
