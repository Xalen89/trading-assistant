# Order block retest audit (owner-only paper app)

**Scope/status.** Audited only Order Block Retest. The preset is explicitly a `reference` sourced from user-provided material, not a proprietary strategy (`lib/trading/setup-presets.ts:5-6,120-160`). Unlike prose alone, `lib/trading/setup-engine.ts:91-97,144-174,207-253,453-495,527-629` contains deterministic closed-bar detectors: bars are filtered to completed as-of time; swing pivots require two later closed confirmations; sweep/rejection, directional body >= prior ATR plus MSS, last opposite candle body/CE, later CE touch, zone invalidation, and readiness are computed. Automatic reference execution is limited to 1m-15m (`setup-engine.ts:1090-1104`). OB logic uses OHLC—not volume/ticks. It is not proof of an exchange-defined institutional order block.

**Confluence candidates (unvalidated; source = selected instrument's provider OHLC, as-of only):** (1) side-matched context: last six completed 1h closes rising/falling (`setup-engine.ts:176-205`); (2) confirmed swing sweep: wick crosses prior pivot then same closed bar closes back across (`:144-225`); (3) post-sweep directional body >= prior 14-bar ATR and close through local structural extreme (`:100-119,228-253`); (4) OB: last opposite candle body between sweep and MSS; later closed bar touches body midpoint, with no subsequent close invalidating the zone (`:453-495,527-595`). Candidate trigger: that later CE touch; require closed-bar evidence and fresh source. A possible test configuration—not a recommendation—is equal configurable weights, 3/4 gate, with trigger mandatory; current detector instead requires the full ordered sequence. No correlation screen or source-specific calibrated weights/gate exists.

**Feed/data limits.** Actual sources are selected-account TradeLocker REST bid/ask and history, plus public Coinbase candles/ticker (`server/trading/tradelocker.ts:29-42,70-72`; `lib/trading/client.ts:11-14,28-50`; `server/trading/market.ts:16,30`). Broker quotes are polled/cached, timestamped on receipt; candles are broker-defined and polled—not exchange WebSocket data. Provider OHLC volume units are unverified, not exchange volume (`lib/trading/indicators.ts:167-173`). No confirmed L2, NBBO, or >=100 real setup outcomes. Need timestamped point-in-time bars, event/zone/retest labels, execution/spread/fees/slippage and intrabar outcomes; no L2/NBBO-derived claims without those feeds.

**Validation gate.** `setup-engine.ts:870-973,1245-1269` permits at most 100 non-overlapping cases, with 12-bar outcomes, >=30 resolved before displaying a descriptive rate, no execution costs and probability null. `python/research_engine.py:14-18,38-54,90-120` supports only MA-cross/FVG—not OB. Before any edge claim: >=100 real eligible outcomes; preregistered definitions/weights; chronological train/validation and rolling walk-forward; untouched final holdout; costs/fills; multiple-hypothesis correction; report condition-level uncertainty and correlated conditions. No such validation is established here.
