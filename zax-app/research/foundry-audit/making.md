# Market-making audit (owner-only paper app)

**Finding.** Market making is not implemented. Closest is `scalping`, a reference-only preset describing L2/tape buyer wall → passive bid, 2–5 tick stop and 3–10 tick target (`lib/trading/indicator-presets.ts:180–215`). Its engine marks tick/microstructure, buyer wall, queue fill and tick size **unknown**; no DMA order exists (`lib/trading/indicator-setups.ts:58–81`). This is prose/reference, not a market-making detector. Generic weighted factors (`lib/trading/engine.ts:15`) do not create a market-making gate. Proposals are simulated, not broker orders (`server/trading/setup-service.ts:246–313`; `server/trading/tradelocker.ts:39–42,72`).

**Data boundary.** Owner-gated TradeLocker provides selected-account REST bid/ask midpoint and broker-defined history; quotes have receipt, not provider, timestamps; bars are polled, not exchange-streamed (`server/trading/tradelocker.ts:21–30,44–72`). Coinbase provides public ticker/REST candles and ticker-only WebSocket—not connected L2 (`server/trading/market.ts:14–18,21–32`; `lib/trading/client.ts:28–35`). Missing: NBBO, timestamped L2/market-by-order, aggressor prints, queue/fill history, tick/lot metadata. Broker candle volume is provider-defined/possibly indicative, not verified exchange traded volume; Coinbase volume is asset volume (`lib/trading/indicators.ts:167–173`). No >=100 outcomes evidenced.

**Unvalidated candidates** (hypotheses only; timestamped exchange data required): (1) spread less maker fees and adverse-selection allowance >0 at quote time; (2) top-five-level depth imbalance above pre-registered threshold in 3 consecutive 100-ms L2 snapshots; (3) same-side best-level size persists ≥500 ms without price retreat; (4) signed aggressor volume favors quote side over prior 1 s; (5) inventory below preset cap and proposed quote reduces absolute inventory. None are active/validated. Choose venue, post-only rule, quote offsets, refresh/cancel latency, fee/tick/lot rounding, inventory skew/cap and queue-fill model before testing; specs/code do not define these.

**Gates.** Require ≥100 independent resolved outcomes or label insufficient; chronological walk-forward; latest untouched holdout used once; realistic fees/rebates, spread, latency, queue priority, partial fills, cancellations and adverse selection; multiple-hypothesis correction; pre-registered weights/gate and flag >0.9-correlated conditions. Promote only with positive net OOS expectancy and holdout confirmation, reporting per-condition sample sizes. Existing research engine supports only MA-cross/FVG (`python/research_engine.py:18,120`).
