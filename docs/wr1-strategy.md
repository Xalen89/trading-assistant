# WR1 Trading Strategy

## Fundamentals & PO3

### Power of 3 (PO3)
Candles Open, Manipulate, Distribute, then Close.

**Bullish Pattern:**
- Open → Manipulate down → Distribute up → Close bullish

**Bearish Pattern:**
- Open → Manipulate up → Distribute down → Close bearish

## Optimal Trading Times (EST)

- **2:00 AM - 3:15 AM** (London)
- **4:00 AM - 4:45 AM** (London)
- **9:30 AM - 11:15 AM** (NY AM)
- Reversals/retracements often occur around **08:30, 09:30, and 10:30**

## Ranges

Defined by Point A and Point B for Fibonacci placement. Types include:
- Swing-to-swing
- Candles (4H, 1H, 15M)
- Wicks

Fibonacci levels: OTE, Quarters, STDV

## Turtle Soup

Sweeping a high/low into a bearish/bullish PD array followed by distribution. Highly effective when paired with:
- SMT
- Wyckoff spring schematics

## Confluence Factors

### Core Levels
- **FVG C.E.** - Fair Value Gap midpoints (C.E. = "Middle")
- **IFVG C.E.** - Inverse Fair Value Gap midpoints
- **RDRB** - Re-deliver Re-balance
- **Overlap C.E. (HIPPOs)** - Overlapping candle wicks
- **Order Block C.E. and Body**
- **Wick C.E.** - Large wick midpoints
- **HTF accumulation candles** - Stubby candle with big wicks on both sides
- **STDV** - Standard Deviation projections (-1 to -4.5)

### Fibonacci Levels
- **Quarters**: 0.25, 0.5, 0.75
- **Midpoints**: 0.375, 0.625
- **OTE levels**: 0.618, 0.705, 0.79

## Extras, Bias, and High Quality Levels

### SMT / C-SMT
Discrepancy where one correlated pair takes a high/low while another does not:
- NQ with ES
- EURUSD with GBPUSD
- NQ with CFD NAS100

### Injections & MOP
- **Volatility injections** at major opens/news events
- **Midnight Opening Price (MOP)** (00:00) - acts as a strong reaction level within a month's lookback

### Time-Based XX
Highs/lows forming at times ending in **X0, X1, X5, X6, or X8** carry higher probability for reactions and turtle soups.

### Bias
Top-down analysis utilizing:
- Monthly, weekly, and daily trends
- Judas swings
- Weekly range profiles (Tradinator33 templates)
- Economic calendars (CPI/PPI on Tuesdays)
- COT commercial positioning
- Seasonality charts
- DXY/Bond correlations

### High Quality Positioning Levels
Formed by aligning multiple confluences:
- Quarters
- OTE
- STDV
- FVG C.E.
- MOP

These confluences create clear, simplified zones.
